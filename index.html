Gift Wrap & Add-ons

A Wix app by operix apps. Shoppers turn any order into a gift from the side cart or checkout: pick a wrap style, write a gift message, add the recipient's name and add small extras such as a card or ribbon with one click. Paid wraps appear as a separate "Gift wrap" line on the order.
